# Yangon Technological University (YTU)

YTU is the inspire university. Welcome to the official overview of academic departments and majors, compiled in alignment with the Myanmar Ministry of Science and Technology official curriculum standards.

---

## 📅 General Program Information

- **Estimated Duration:** 5 Years (Undergraduate Bachelor's Degree)
- **Academic System:** Semester-based with practical workshop/field training and a final year capstone project.

---

## 🏛️ Architecture & Engineering Majors

### 1. Architecture

- **Overview:** Focuses on the art and science of designing buildings and environments. Students learn spatial design, urban planning, architectural history, and sustainable building technologies.
- **Core Courses:** Architectural Design, History of Architecture, Building Materials & Construction, Environmental Science in Architecture, Urban Planning.
- **Popular Careers:** Architect, Urban Planner, Interior Designer, Project Manager, Landscape Architect.

### 2. Chemical Engineering

- **Overview:** Combines physical sciences and life sciences with mathematics to transform raw materials into valuable products safely and sustainably.
- **Core Courses:** Chemical Process Principles, Fluid Mechanics, Heat & Mass Transfer, Chemical Reaction Engineering, Plant Design.
- **Popular Careers:** Chemical Process Engineer, Petroleum Refinery Engineer, Quality Control Manager, Food & Beverage Process Engineer.

### 3. Civil Engineering

- **Overview:** Deals with the design, construction, and maintenance of the physical and naturally built environment, including infrastructure like roads, bridges, and dams.
- **Core Courses:** Structural Analysis, Fluid Mechanics, Soil Mechanics & Foundation Engineering, Reinforced Concrete Design, Environmental Engineering.
- **Popular Careers:** Civil Engineer, Structural Engineer, Site Manager, Geotechnical Engineer, Infrastructure Consultant.

### 4. Communication Engineering

- **Overview:** Focuses on telecommunication networks, data transmission, wireless systems, and the infrastructure needed to support modern communications.
- **Core Courses:** Signal and Systems, Digital Communications, Antenna and Wave Propagation, Mobile Communication Systems, Fiber Optic Networks.
- **Popular Careers:** Telecom Engineer, Network Administrator, Radio Frequency (RF) Engineer, Systems Engineer.

### 5. Electrical Power Engineering

- **Overview:** Concentrates on the generation, transmission, distribution, and utilization of electrical energy, emphasizing power grid safety and renewable energy sources.
- **Core Courses:** Electrical Machines, Power System Analysis, High Voltage Engineering, Power Electronics, Power System Protection.
- **Popular Careers:** Power Grid Engineer, Electrical Design Engineer, Maintenance Engineer, Renewable Energy Consultant.

### 6. Electronic Engineering

- **Overview:** Focuses on electronic circuits, semiconductor devices, embedded systems, and microprocessors used in automation and consumer electronics.
- **Core Courses:** Analog & Digital Circuit Design, Microprocessor & Microcontrollers, Electromagnetic Fields, Electronic Circuit Analysis.
- **Popular Careers:** Electronics Engineer, Embedded Systems Developer, Hardware Design Engineer, Automation Specialist.

### 7. Computer Engineering and Information Technology

- **Overview:** Bridges hardware engineering and software development to design smart computer systems, networks, and advanced software solutions.
- **Core Courses:** Data Structures & Algorithms, Database Management Systems, Computer Architecture, Operating Systems, Software Engineering.
- **Popular Careers:** Software Engineer, Full-Stack Developer, Network Engineer, IT Consultant, Database Administrator.

### 8. Mechanical Engineering

- **Overview:** One of the broadest disciplines, focusing on the design, analysis, manufacturing, and maintenance of mechanical and thermal systems.
- **Core Courses:** Thermodynamics, Fluid Mechanics, Mechanics of Machines, Machine Design, Heat Transfer.
- **Popular Careers:** Mechanical Engineer, HVAC Engineer, Plant Maintenance Engineer, Automotive Engineer.

### 9. Mechatronic Engineering

- **Overview:** An interdisciplinary field combining mechanical engineering, electronics, computer engineering, and control systems to design automated and robotic machinery.
- **Core Courses:** Robotics and Automation, Control Systems, Sensors and Actuators, PLC Programming, Microcontroller Applications.
- **Popular Careers:** Automation Engineer, Robotics Specialist, Control Systems Engineer, Instrumentation Engineer.

### 10. Metallurgical Engineering

- **Overview:** Studies the physical and chemical behavior of metallic elements, their intermetallic compounds, and their mixtures (alloys).
- **Core Courses:** Physical Metallurgy, Extraction Metallurgy, Material Science, Foundry Engineering, Corrosion Engineering.
- **Popular Careers:** Metallurgist, Materials Engineer, Quality Assurance Engineer, Welding Engineer.

### 11. Mining Engineering

- **Overview:** Covers the extraction of minerals from underneath, above, or within the ground, focusing on safety, economic viability, and environmental sustainability.
- **Core Courses:** Surface Mining, Underground Mining Technology, Rock Mechanics, Mineral Processing, Mine Safety & Management.
- **Popular Careers:** Mining Engineer, Mine Manager, Drilling Engineer, Geotechnical Consultant.

### 12. Petroleum Engineering

- **Overview:** Focuses on the exploration, drilling, extraction, and production of oil and natural gas resources.
- **Core Courses:** Drilling Engineering, Reservoir Engineering, Petroleum Production Engineering, Well Logging, Well Testing.
- **Popular Careers:** Petroleum Engineer, Reservoir Engineer, Drilling Supervisor, Production Analyst.

### 13. Textile Engineering

- **Overview:** Applies engineering principles to the design, production, and control of fiber, textile, and apparel processes, products, and machinery.
- **Core Courses:** Fiber Science, Yarn Manufacturing, Fabric Manufacturing, Textile Chemistry & Wet Processing, Garment Manufacturing Technology.
- **Popular Careers:** Textile Engineer, Production Manager, Quality Control Specialist, Apparel Product Developer.

## 📝 Supporting Academic Departments

The following departments do not offer standalone engineering degrees but provide core foundational, scientific, and language curriculum required across all 5-year engineering paths:

- **Geology:** Provides critical groundwork for Civil, Mining, and Petroleum engineering majors.
- **Chemistry:** Foundation for Chemical, Metallurgical, and Textile engineering programs.
- **English:** Technical report writing, presentation skills, and professional communication.
- **Mathematics:** The core computational foundation for all engineering disciplines.
- **Myanmar:** Professional correspondence, national cultural studies, and ethics.
- **Physics:** Fundamental physical principles applied in Electrical, Mechanical, and Electronic tracks.
